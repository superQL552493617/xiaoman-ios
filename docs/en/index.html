Xiaoman · Help & Support

Xiaoman is a simple, gentle habit tracker and journal: light up a few small habits each day, write a few lines, and watch your days slowly fill up.

FAQ

Where is my data stored?
Everything stays on your phone; we have no servers. You can export a backup in “Me → Backup”.

What about a new phone?
If you migrate your whole iPhone or restore from iCloud Backup, your data comes along. You can also export a backup on the old phone and import it on the new one.

How do I restore Premium?
Tap “Restore purchases” in “Me → Get Premium” using the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel auto-renewal?
Turn it off in iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
